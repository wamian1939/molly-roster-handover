# Hurstville opening shifts and official-score preference

The current Hurstville Lab rule is `hurstville:v12`. Each weekday has a separate `开早` pattern count of 3. That pattern starts at 10:00 and may finish at 16:00 or 17:00. The former `Early` pattern now starts at 11:00; its weekday counts were reduced by 3 so the total early staffing targets were preserved. `Full` and `Late` counts were unchanged. Lab SQLite was backed up before the `v11` and `v12` saves in `.data/backups/`.

The real-source adapter reads `officialScore.score` from Evaluate's authenticated roster-integration employee API. Evaluate defines it as the latest valid CLOSED-round aggregate. The pattern-count solver uses the unrounded official score (scaled to tenths for the integer objective) as a separate preference tier after pattern gaps and before work efficiency and fairness. Missing scores do not receive an invented value. Availability, formal position/skill qualification, leave supplied for the run, overlap constraints and exact pattern counts still govern eligibility. Historical non-pattern rules retain their previous score weighting.
